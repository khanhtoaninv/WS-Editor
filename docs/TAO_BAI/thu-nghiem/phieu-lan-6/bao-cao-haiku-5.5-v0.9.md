# BÁO CÁO: Daily Routines & Activities (GRAM-MOVERS-DAILY-01)

## NOTES

Task 1: Students write the verb only, with no object. Accept "plays", "eat", "is drawing". The "They / Now" cell is pre-printed "—" and is not scored.

Task 2: Accept only the marked answers. Items 1 and 3 rely on the scene line and the reply, not on time words. Item 2 is a habit answer to a question about hobbies.

Task 3: Answers are marked inline with "*". Item 4 is "at the moment". Items 2 and 3 use replies that signal the present moment ("Sorry", "I can't").

Task 4: Accept "do not go" or "don't go" for item 3. Accept only "Do / watch" for item 4 and "Does / eat" for item 5.

Task 5 (model answers, accept variants):
- Ben: "Ben plays football on Saturday at 10 a.m. Now he is drawing a picture for Grandma."
- Lucy: "Lucy swims at the sports centre on Saturday at 9 a.m. She is reading a story in the garden now."
Criteria: each sentence separates the Saturday routine from the Sunday 3 p.m. action, and the friend's mistaken "now" is corrected.

Task 6 (model answers, not scored):
- "I usually go to the park on Sundays. My mum and I are cooking lunch now."
Criteria: two Present Simple sentences with a habit meaning, two Present Continuous sentences with a now meaning, all true for the student.
