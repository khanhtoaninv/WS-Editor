## Task: Copy the verbs into the table
Hướng dẫn: Read about Ben and Lucy. Write what they do and what they are doing in the table.
Dạng: bảng
Khối chữ trên:

  At twelve o'clock, they eat lunch together.
  It is Sunday, three o'clock in the afternoon.
  They are at Grandma's house.
  Ben is drawing a picture for Grandma, and Lucy is reading a story in the garden.
  Grandma is looking at them through the window.
Name | Usually (what someone does again and again) | Now (what someone is doing at this moment)
