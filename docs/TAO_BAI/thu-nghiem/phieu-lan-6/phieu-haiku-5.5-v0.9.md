# Grammar Worksheet: Daily Routines & Activities
Tuổi: 7-9
Xếp trang: tách

## Task: Copy the verbs into the table
Hướng dẫn: Read about Ben and Lucy. Write what they do and what they are doing in the table.
Dạng: bảng
Khối chữ trên:
  On Saturdays, Lucy swims at the sports centre at 9 o'clock.
  On Saturdays, Ben plays football in the park at 10 o'clock.
  On Saturdays, Ben and Lucy eat lunch together at 12 o'clock.
  On Sunday at 3 p.m., Ben is drawing a picture for Grandma.
  At the same time, Lucy is reading a story in the garden.
  On Sundays, they usually go to Grandma's house.
Name | Usually (what someone does again and again) | Now (what someone is doing at this moment)
Lucy | swims | is reading
Ben | =plays | =is drawing
They | =eat | —

## Task: Choose the correct answer
Hướng dẫn: Read the conversations. Circle the correct answers.
Dạng: chọn A/B
0. Tom: What do you do on Sundays, Mia?
   Mia: ___
   A. I ride my bike in the park.

   Mia: Can I talk to Grandma, Tom?
   Tom: ___
   A. Sorry, she is cooking lunch.

   Mia: ___
   A. I am playing football.

5. Mia: What does Grandma do on Sundays, Tom?
   Tom: ___
   A. She is cooking lunch for us.
   B. She cooks lunch for us.

## Task: Choose the correct words
Hướng dẫn: Circle the correct words.
Dạng: chọn trong câu
0. On Sundays, Mia {is riding|*rides} her bike.
1. Right now, I {*am drawing|draw} a picture.
2. Tom: Can I talk to Dad?
   Mum: Sorry, he {*is cooking|cooks} dinner.
3. Tom: Can you come to the park, Mia?
   Mia: No, I can't. I {*am doing|do} my homework.
4. {Does Tom play|*Is Tom playing} football at the moment?
5. {*Does Grandma cook|Is Grandma cooking} lunch on Sundays?
6. Tom {*doesn't watch|isn't watching} television on Sundays. He goes to the park.

## Task: Tell your friend

Dạng: bảng

## Task: Now it's your turn
Hướng dẫn: Write two sentences about what you usually do on Sundays. Then write two sentences about what you and someone in your family are doing now. Make sure they are true.
Khung từ: play, read, watch, eat, go, draw, ride, swim

  My sister is reading a book now.
Dạng: viết tự do
