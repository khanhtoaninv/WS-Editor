# Daily Routines and Activities
Tuổi: 7-9
Xếp trang: tách

## Task: Copy the verbs into the table
Hướng dẫn: Read about Ben and Lucy. Write what they do and what they are doing in the table.
Dạng: bảng
Khối chữ trên:
  On Saturdays, Lucy swims at the sports centre at 9 a.m.
  On Saturdays, Ben plays football in the park at 10 a.m.
  On Saturdays, the two of them eat lunch together at 12 p.m.
  On Sunday at 3 p.m., Ben is drawing a picture for Grandma.
  Meanwhile, Lucy is reading a story in the garden.

## Task: Complete the sentences
Hướng dẫn: Write the verb in the right way. Use the word in ( ).
Dạng: điền từ
0. Tom and Mia ___ (play) football on Saturdays.
1. Tom: Sorry, Mum. I ___ (draw) a picture.
2. ___ you ___ (play) tennis after school?
3. Mia ___ (not swim) on Mondays.
4. Mum: Is Dad busy?
   Tom: Yes! He ___ (eat) his lunch.
5. Mia and Tom ___ (go) to the park on Sundays.

## Task: Now it's your turn
Hướng dẫn: Write two sentences about what you usually do on Sundays. Then write two sentences about what you and someone in your family are doing now. Make sure they are true.
Khung từ: read, watch, eat, go, draw, ride, swim, play
Khối chữ trên:
  Usually, I go to the park with my dad on Sundays.
  Right now, my mum is cooking lunch.
Dạng: viết tự do
Dòng viết: 4
