## Task: Copy the words into the table
Hướng dẫn: Read the text. Write the words in the table.
Khối chữ trên:
  Ben is taller than Lucy.
  Ben is the tallest of the three.
  Ben is older than Lucy.
  Ben is the oldest of the three.
  Sam is faster than Ben.
  Sam is the fastest of the three.
Dạng: bảng
Word | Two things (Ben is taller than Lucy.) | Three or more (Ben is the tallest.)
tall | taller than | the tallest
old | =older than | =the oldest
fast | =faster than | =the fastest

## Task: Circle the correct words.
Hướng dẫn: Circle the correct words.
Dạng: chọn trong câu
0. The elephant is {heavy than|*heavier than|more heavy than} the dog.
1. The giraffe is {the tallest|*taller than|more tall} the horse.
2. {*Grandma is the oldest in my family.|Grandma is older in my family.|Grandma is oldest in my family.}
3. The cat is {*faster than|fast than|more fast} the mouse.
4. Tom: Who is {*the fastest|faster|fast} in the class?
   Mia: Sam is the fastest of the three.
5. Scene: Lucy ran 50 metres in 10 seconds. Ben ran in 11 seconds.
   Mia: Who ran {the slowest|*faster than|more fast} Ben?
   Tom: Lucy did.
