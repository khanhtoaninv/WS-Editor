## Task: Choose the correct answer
Hướng dẫn: Read the conversations. Circle the correct answers.
Dạng: chọn A/B/C
0. Mia: What do you do on Saturdays, Tom?
   Tom: ___

   C. I cycles to the shop.

   Tom: Mia, is your dog awake?

   B. It sleep under the table.

   Tom: Mia, what are you doing?
   Mia: I ___
   A. I count cars.
   B. I am count cars.
   C. I am counting cars.

   Tom: He ___
   A. He is cooking pancakes.
   B. He cooks pancakes.
   C. He cook pancakes.
