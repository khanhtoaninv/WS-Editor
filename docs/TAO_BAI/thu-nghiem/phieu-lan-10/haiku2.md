## Task: Copy the verbs into the table
Hướng dẫn: Read about Ben and Lucy. Write what they do and what they are doing in the table.
Dạng: bảng

  On Saturdays, Lucy swims at the sports centre at 9 a.m.
  On Saturdays, Ben plays football in the park at 10 a.m.
  On Saturdays, Ben and Lucy eat lunch together at 12 p.m.
  On Sunday, 3 p.m., Ben is drawing a picture for Grandma.
  At the same time, Lucy is reading a story in the garden.

## Task: Complete the sentences

Dạng: điền từ

   Tom: Sorry, Mum. I'm busy. I ___ (read) a story.

3. Mia ___ (not swim) on Sundays.
4. Mum: Are you busy, Mia?
   Mia: Yes! I ___ (draw) a picture for Grandma.
5. Tom and Mia ___ (eat) lunch together on Saturdays.

## Task: Tell your friend
Hướng dẫn: Read about Ben and Lucy. Then write sentences to answer your friend.
Dạng: bảng
Khối chữ trên:
  It is Sunday, 3 p.m.
Name | Usually (what someone does again and again) | Now (what someone is doing at this moment)

   Ben … He …

   Lucy … She …

## Task: Now it's your turn
Hướng dẫn: Write two sentences about what you usually do on Sundays. Then write two sentences about what you and someone in your family are doing now. Make sure they are true.
Khung từ: play, read, watch, eat, go, draw, ride, swim
Khối chữ trên:
  I usually ride my bike in the park on Sundays.
  My sister is drawing a picture in the garden.
Dạng: viết tự do
Dòng viết: 4
