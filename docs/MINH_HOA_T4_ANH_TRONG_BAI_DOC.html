T4 — Ảnh trong bài đọc: các phương án bố trí

Mỗi khung là một Task: G0 tiêu đề, G1 ảnh, G2 bài đọc (cùng một khối), G3 câu hỏi ở dưới. đã có ở v93 đề xuất thêm

1. Bao quanh, ảnh phải đã có

Ảnh float phải, chữ chạy bên trái rồi tràn xuống dưới ảnh. Kiểu báo chí.

1. Read and answer.
G1 ảnh
The city council met on Monday to discuss a new plan for the old river park. Many residents said they wanted more trees and fewer cars near the water. The mayor promised that work would begin next spring and that the park would stay open during the building. Some shop owners were worried about noise, but most people agreed the change was good for the town. Children from the nearby school will help plant the first trees in April, and local artists will paint a long wall beside the path.
1. When will work begin?
2. Who will plant the trees?
