Bài 3 · Match the questions and answers.

⚠ Kiểm tra: câu 4 và câu 6 cùng ghép được với "Yes, she was." → đáp án không duy nhất.
Mục tiêuHỏi và trả lời Was he…? / Were they…? với câu trả lời ngắn.Ngữ cảnhHỏi về các bạn trong lớp sau chuyến dã ngoại Chủ nhật.

Match the questions and answers.

1. Was Ben at the picnic?a. Yes, they were.
2. Were Lan and Mai tired?b. No, he wasn't. He was ill.
3. Was the weather nice?c. Yes, it was. It was sunny.
4. Was Ms Hoa with you?d. Yes, she was.
5. Were you hungry?e. Yes, I was.
6. Was your sister happy?f. Yes, she was. She loved it.

Đáp án: 1-b · 2-a · 3-c · 4-d · 5-e · 6-f
