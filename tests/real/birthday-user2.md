## Task: Read & Choose the Main Idea
Hướng dẫn: Read the story. Then choose the best summary (A, B or C).
Trả lời: khoanh A/B/C
Bài đọc:
On Saturday morning, Lily was at home with her mum. It was her birthday, but she thought everyone was busy. At ten o'clock, her friends Ben, Amy and Jack were at Ben's house. They were making a surprise party for Lily. Amy put balloons near the window. Jack made sandwiches, and Ben put a chocolate cake on the table. Lily's dad brought some presents.

At two o'clock, Lily's mum said, "Let's visit Ben." When Lily opened the door, the room was dark. She couldn't see her friends. Suddenly, the lights came on, and everyone shouted, "Happy birthday!" Lily was very surprised.

But then their dog Max jumped onto a chair and took a sandwich! Everyone laughed. Ben gave Max his own small biscuit, so he left the food alone. They ate cake, played games and sang songs. Lily smiled because her friends made her day special.

1. A. Lily and her friends made a cake at school.
   B. Lily’s friends planned a party, and something funny happened.
   C. Lily went to a party, but no one came.
