# Weekend with Ben and Lucy – Present Simple and Present Continuous (Movers)
Tuổi: 7-9
Xếp trang: liền
Khoảng cách: vừa

## Task: Read and underline.
Hướng dẫn: Read about Ben and Lucy. Underline the verbs in blue (usual things) and red (things happening at that moment).
Trả lời: không
Ảnh: Ben and Lucy at the weekend
Bài đọc:
Ben and Lucy are brother and sister. They love weekends! On Saturdays, Ben plays football in the park and Lucy swims at the sports centre. On Sundays, they usually visit their grandma.
It is Sunday afternoon now. Ben is drawing a picture for Grandma. Lucy is reading a story in the garden, and Grandma is watching them from the window.

## Task: Habit or happening now?
Hướng dẫn: Read about Ben and Lucy. Circle Habit (they do it often) or Now (it is happening at this moment).
Dạng: đúng/sai (Habit/Now, cùng hàng)
0. Ben takes his football to the park every Saturday.
1. Lucy has a call from Grandma. She is talking to her about the weekend.
2. Ben and Lucy ride their bikes to the sports centre on Saturdays.
3. Listen! Ben is playing the guitar in his room.
4. Lucy draws a cat in her notebook after school.
5. Look at Lucy! She is eating an ice cream.
6. Ben watches cartoons with his sister on Sunday mornings.
7. Ben and Lucy are making pancakes in the kitchen at the moment.

## Task: Choose the correct verb.
Hướng dẫn: Circle the correct words.
Dạng: chọn trong câu
1. Ben {*plays|is playing} football in the park every Saturday.
2. Look! Lucy {swims|*is swimming} in the big pool.
3. My brother {*doesn't read|isn't reading} books on Sundays; he plays games.
4. Grandma {cooks|*is cooking} lunch in the kitchen at the moment.
5. {*Does|Is} Lucy go to the sports centre on Saturdays?
6. {Do|*Are} Ben and Lucy watching TV now?

## Task: Complete the sentences.
Hướng dẫn: Write the correct form of the verb in brackets.
Dạng: điền từ
0. Ben ___ (draw) pictures every Sunday.
1. Lucy ___ (read) a book in the garden now.
2. They ___ (not play) football on Sundays.
3. What ___ Ben ___ (do) at the moment?
4. ___ Lucy ___ (swim) on Saturdays?
5. Ben and Lucy ___ (not watch) TV now. They are in the garden.

## Task: Look at the information and write sentences.
Hướng dẫn: Read about Lucy's weekend. Then answer the questions with a full sentence.
Khối chữ trên:
  Lucy – Saturdays: 9 o'clock swim at the sports centre · 12 o'clock eat lunch with Ben.
  Lucy – Right now (Sunday, 3 o'clock): reading a story in the garden. Ben is drawing a picture.
Dạng: viết câu
Dòng viết: 2
1. What does Lucy usually do at 9 o'clock on Saturdays?
2. Who does Lucy eat lunch with on Saturdays?
3. What is Lucy doing now?
4. What is Ben doing now?
