# Weekend with Ben and Lucy – Present Simple and Present Continuous (Movers)
Tuổi: 7-9
Xếp trang: tách
Khoảng cách: vừa

## Task: Read and underline.
Hướng dẫn: Look at Ben and Lucy. Underline the action words (verbs). Use a straight line for things they do every week. Use a wavy line for things they are doing now.
Trả lời: không
Ảnh: Ben and Lucy at Grandma's house on Sunday
Bài đọc:
On Saturdays, Ben plays football in the park and Lucy swims at the sports centre. On Sundays, they usually visit Grandma.
Look at them now! Ben is drawing a picture for Grandma. Lucy is reading a story in the garden. Grandma is watching them from the window.
Khối chữ trên:
  Example: Ben <u>plays</u> football in the park. (straight line)

## Task: Often or now?
Hướng dẫn: Read about Tom, Mia and their family. Circle Often (it happens again and again) or Now (it is happening at this moment).
Dạng: đúng/sai (Often/Now, cùng hàng)
0. Tom takes his football to the park every Saturday.
1. Mia's phone is ringing. She is talking to her grandma.
2. Mia's mum teaches English at a big school.
3. The kitchen smells great. Tom's dad is making pancakes.
4. Look at Tom! He is eating an ice cream.
5. Tom's dog sleeps in a basket by the door.
6. Mia draws a cat in her notebook after school.
7. Mia's sister is sitting at the desk. She is writing a card.
8. Their grandma knits scarves for the whole family.

## Task: Choose the correct words.
Hướng dẫn: Circle the correct words.
Dạng: chọn trong câu
0. Tom {*plays|is playing} football in the park every Saturday.
1. Tom is at the zoo with his class. A big monkey {eats|*is eating} a banana.
2. Mia's dad is a zoo keeper. He {*feeds|is feeding} the monkeys at 8 o'clock every morning.
3. Tom {*doesn't swim|isn't swimming} on Mondays. He plays tennis.
4. "Mia, where are you?" "I {*am looking|look} at the elephants!"
5. Mia is drawing a monkey. {Does her brother draw|*Is her brother drawing} too?
6. {*Do Tom and Mia ride|Are Tom and Mia riding} their bikes to school every day?

## Task: Complete the sentences.
Hướng dẫn: Write the correct form of the verb in brackets.
Dạng: điền từ
0. Tom ___ (draw) pictures every Sunday.
1. Look! Mia ___ (read) a book in the garden.
2. Tom and Mia ___ (not play) football on Sundays.
3. "What ___ you ___ (do), Tom?" "I'm drawing a monkey."
4. ___ Mia ___ (swim) at the sports centre? Yes, she does. She goes every Saturday.
5. Tom and Mia ___ (not watch) TV. They ___ (play) football in the garden.

## Task: Tell your friend.
Hướng dẫn: Read about Ben and Lucy. Then write two sentences to answer your friend. Use the correct tenses.
Khối chữ trên:
  Lucy: on Saturdays → 9 o'clock: swim at the sports centre. Now (Sunday, 3 o'clock) → read a story in the garden.
  Ben: on Saturdays → 10 o'clock: play football in the park. Now (Sunday, 3 o'clock) → draw a picture for Grandma.
Dạng: viết câu
Dòng viết: 2
1. Sam says: "Lucy is swimming at the sports centre now." Tell Sam what Lucy usually does on Saturdays and what she is doing now.
2. Sam says: "Ben is playing football in the park now." Tell Sam what Ben usually does on Saturdays and what he is doing now.

## Task: Now it's your turn.
Hướng dẫn: Write two sentences about what you usually do on Sundays and two sentences about what you are doing now. Make sure they are true about you.
Khung từ: play, read, watch, eat, go, draw, ride, swim
Khối chữ trên:
  Example: I usually play with my dog on Sundays. Now I am writing in my notebook.
Dạng: viết tự do
Dòng viết: 4
