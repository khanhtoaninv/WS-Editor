## Task: Synonyms and antonyms
Hướng dẫn: Mark the letter A, B, C or D on your answer sheet to indicate the word(s) CLOSEST in meaning to the underlined word(s) in each of the following questions.
Trả lời: khoanh A/B/C
Dạng: chọn A/B/C
1. Acupuncture <u>originated</u> in China and has been used as a traditional medicine for thousands of years. A. began B. created C. developed D. introduced
2. There is no evidence at this time that acupuncture can treat cancer itself. A. clue B. data C. proof D. sign

### Phần: Choose the correct answer.
Hướng dẫn: Mark the letter A, B, C or D indicate the word(s) OPPOSITE in meaning to the underlined word(s) in each of the following questions.
Trả lời: khoanh A/B/C
Dạng: chọn A/B/C
1. The gender gap in primary education has been eliminated. A. variety B. inconsistency C. difference D. similarity
2. The United Kingdom has made remarkable progress in gender equality. A. insignificant B. impressive C. notable D. famous
