Bài đọc:
On Saturday, Max and his class visited Sunny Farm. They went there by bus. First, they fed the goats and watched two baby rabbits. Max wanted to see the horses, but the horses were in another field. At twelve o'clock, the children sat under a big tree for lunch. Lily opened her bag, but her lunchbox was empty. She had left her sandwich at home. Max gave her half of his cheese sandwich. Lily smiled and said, 'Thank you!' After lunch, it began to rain. The teacher took everyone into a small barn. They listened to the rain and drew pictures of the animals. When the rain stopped, they went home. Max's favourite part of the trip was feeding the goats.
