## Task: [T32] Bài đọc dài (thử lật Task dài, phóng, cuộn)
Hướng dẫn: Read and answer.
Dạng: viết câu
Dòng viết: 2
Bài đọc:
**The Little Fox**
Once upon a time, a little fox lived at the edge of a big forest. Every morning he woke up early and ran to the river to drink water. He liked to watch the fish swim under the bridge.

One day, the fox saw a red ball floating on the river. He jumped into the water and pulled it out. The ball belonged to a girl named Mia who lived in the village nearby.

Mia was very happy to get her ball back. She gave the fox a piece of bread, and from that day they became good friends.

The next summer, Mia and her family went on a trip to the mountains. The little fox missed her very much. Every evening he sat on the hill and looked at the road.

When autumn came, Mia returned. The fox ran to meet her, and they played together until the sun went down.

1. Where did the little fox live?
2. What did the fox pull out of the river?
3. Who was Mia?
4. What did Mia give the fox?
5. How did the fox feel when Mia went away?
6. What happened when autumn came?
