# GĐ0 · Phiếu mẫu đủ dạng có đáp án
Tuổi: 10-12

## Task: Complete the sentences.
Hướng dẫn: Write one word in each gap.
Dạng: điền từ
1. He ___ to school every day and ___ football after school.
2. She ___ (not) like coffee.
3. My uncle is twenty-___ years old.

## Task: Complete the conversation.
Dạng: điền từ
Hội thoại:
Mai: Hi Ben. Where ___ you yesterday?
Ben: I was at home.
Mai: What did you ___ ?
Ben: I watched a film.

## Task: Read and fill in the gaps.
Bài đọc:
Lucy ___ a cat called Tom. Every morning Tom ___ milk.
Dạng: viết câu
1. What is the cat's name?

## Task: Choose the correct answer.
Dạng: chọn A/B/C
1. She ___ a teacher. A. am B. is C. are
2. Which are fruits? A. apple B. chair C. banana

## Task: Circle the correct word.
Dạng: chọn trong câu
1. She {*goes|go} to school and {play|*plays} football.
2. I {*am|is|are} happy.

## Task: Find the mistake.
Dạng: chọn trong câu (nhãn A-D)
1. She <u>go</u> to <u>school</u> every <u>day</u> by <u>bus</u>.

## Task: True or false?
Dạng: đúng/sai (True/False)
1. The sun is hot.
2. Fish can fly.
