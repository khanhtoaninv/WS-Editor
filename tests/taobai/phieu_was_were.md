# Unit 5 · My weekend · Lesson 2 · was / were
Tuổi: 7-9

## Task: Look and choose.
Dạng: chọn trong câu
1. The children {was|*were} at the park.
2. Tom {*was|were} happy.
3. Lan and Mai {was|*were} on the swings.
4. The weather {*was|were} sunny.
5. The dogs {was|*were} very noisy.
6. Ms Hoa {*was|were} tired.

## Task: Complete with was, were, wasn't or weren't.
Dạng: điền từ
Khung từ: was, were, wasn't, weren't
1. My mum ___ at home on Sunday.
2. We ___ at school. It was Sunday!
3. Ben ___ ill, so he stayed in bed.
4. The shops ___ open in the evening.
5. I ___ hungry after lunch.
6. My friends ___ at the cinema.

## Task: Match the questions and answers.
Dạng: nối
Xáo vế phải: có
1. Was Ben at the picnic? - No, he wasn't. He was ill.
2. Were Lan and Mai tired? - Yes, they were.
3. Was the weather nice? - Yes, it was. It was sunny.
4. Was Ms Hoa with you? - Yes, she was.
5. Were you hungry? - Yes, I was.
6. Was your sister happy? - Yes, she was. She loved it.

## Task: Read and circle T or F.
Bài đọc:
Last Sunday, Mai and her family were at the beach. The weather was hot and sunny. Her brother was in the water all morning. Mai wasn't in the water. She was on the sand with her mum. They were very happy. In the afternoon they were tired, so they were at home early.
Dạng: đúng/sai (cùng hàng)
1. Mai was at the zoo.
2. The weather was hot.
3. Her brother was in the water.
4. Mai was in the water.
5. They were tired in the afternoon.
